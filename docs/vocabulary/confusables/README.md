# 易混词辨析

易混词分两类：一类是 **意思相近**，翻译成中文差不多，用法却不同；另一类是 **拼写相近**，长得像，意思却完全不同。

## 意思相近

### 说：say、speak、talk、tell

| 单词 | 侧重 | 常见用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| say | 说的 **内容** | say hello、say sorry、He said (that) he was tired. |
| speak | 说话的 **能力** 或 **语言**，较正式 | speak English、Can I speak to Tom?（电话用语） |
| talk | 双方 **交谈** | talk to / with sb.、talk about sth. |
| tell | 告诉 **某人** 某事，后接双宾语 | tell sb. sth.、tell a story、tell the truth、tell a lie |

### 看：see、look、watch、read

| 单词 | 侧重 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| see | 看见，强调 **结果** | I can see a bird. |
| look | 看，强调 **动作**，后接宾语时要加 at | Look at the board. |
| watch | 注视 **活动或变化** 的事物 | watch TV、watch a game |
| read | 阅读文字 | read a book、read the newspaper |

### 听：hear、listen

| 单词 | 侧重 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hear | 听见，强调 **结果** | Can you hear me? |
| listen | 听，强调 **动作**，后接宾语时要加 to | Listen to the music. |

### 借：borrow、lend、keep

| 单词 | 方向 | 结构 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| borrow | 借入（从别人那里借来） | borrow sth. from sb. |
| lend | 借出（借给别人） | lend sb. sth. / lend sth. to sb. |
| keep | 借用一段时间（延续性） | How long can I keep the book? |

### 带：bring、take、fetch、carry

| 单词 | 方向 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bring | 从别处带 **到说话人这里** | Bring your homework tomorrow. |
| take | 从说话人这里带 **到别处** | Take an umbrella with you. |
| fetch | 去别处取来，**一去一回** | Can you fetch me some water? |
| carry | 随身携带，不强调方向 | She's carrying a heavy bag. |

### 花费：spend、cost、take、pay

| 单词 | 主语 | 结构 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| spend | 人 | sb. spend time / money on sth.<br>sb. spend time / money (in) doing sth. |
| cost | 物 | sth. cost sb. money |
| take | 形式主语 it | It takes sb. time to do sth. |
| pay | 人 | sb. pay money for sth. |

- I spent two hours on my homework.
- The book cost me 20 dollars.
- It took me two hours to finish my homework.
- I paid 20 dollars for the book.

### 旅行：trip、travel、journey、tour

| 单词 | 侧重 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| trip | 短途的往返旅行，可数 | a business trip、a day trip |
| travel | 泛指旅行这件事，多作不可数名词或动词 | I love travel. / travel abroad |
| journey | 较长的单程旅途，强调路程 | a long journey home |
| tour | 游览多个地点的观光、巡回 | a tour of Europe、a world tour |

### 工作：job、work

job 是可数名词，指一份具体的职业或任务：a good job、find a job。work 作“工作”讲时不可数：I have a lot of work to do. / go to work。

### 升起与躺：rise、raise、lie、lay

| 单词 | 及物性 | 含义 | 变化 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rise | 不及物 | 升起，上涨 | rise – rose – risen |
| raise | 及物 | 举起，提高，抚养 | raise – raised – raised |
| lie | 不及物 | 躺，位于 | lie – lay – lain |
| lie | 不及物 | 说谎 | lie – lied – lied |
| lay | 及物 | 放置，下蛋 | lay – laid – laid |

- The sun rises in the east. / Raise your hand.
- He lay on the bed. / She laid the book on the table.

### 其他

| 词语 | 区别 |
| --- | --- |
| fewer / less | fewer 修饰可数名词复数（fewer people）；less 修饰不可数名词（less water） |
| between / among | between 用于两者之间；among 用于三者或更多之间 |
| beside / besides | beside 在……旁边；besides 除……之外（还有），包括在内 |
| especially / specially | especially 尤其，特别是；specially 专门地，为了特定目的 |
| affect / effect | affect 是动词，影响；effect 通常是名词，效果、影响：affect sb. / have an effect on sb. |
| maybe / may be | maybe 是副词，也许，放句首；may be 是情态动词 + be：Maybe he is right. / He may be right. |
| already / all ready | already 已经；all ready 全都准备好了 |

## 拼写相近

| 单词 | 含义 | 单词 | 含义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| quiet | 安静的 | quite | 相当，十分 |
| accept | 接受 | except | 除……之外（不包括在内） |
| advice | 建议（名词，不可数） | advise | 建议（动词） |
| lose | 丢失 | loose | 松的 |
| adapt | 适应 | adopt | 采用，收养 |
| principal | 校长；主要的 | principle | 原则 |
| complement | 补充 | compliment | 赞美 |
| desert | 沙漠；抛弃 | dessert | 甜点 |
| stationary | 静止的 | stationery | 文具 |
| economic | 经济的 | economical | 节约的 |
| historic | 有历史意义的 | historical | 历史上的，与历史有关的 |
| sensible | 明智的 | sensitive | 敏感的 |
| personal | 个人的 | personnel | 全体员工，人事部门 |
| later | 后来，稍后 | latter | 后者 |
| breath | 呼吸（名词） | breathe | 呼吸（动词） |
| cloth | 布料 | clothes | 衣服 |
| custom | 习俗 | costume | 服装，戏服 |
