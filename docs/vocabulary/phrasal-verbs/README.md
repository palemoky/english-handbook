# 短语动词

短语动词（phrasal verb）由 **动词 + 小品词**（副词或介词）构成，整体意思常常无法从字面推出，如 give up（放弃）、look after（照顾）。口语中短语动词比对应的单个动词更常用：put off 比 postpone 常用，find out 比 discover 常用。

## 四种类型

| 类型 | 说明 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 不及物 | 后面不接宾语 | The car broke down.<br>I get up at seven. |
| 及物、可分 | 宾语可以放在小品词前或后；**宾语是代词时必须放中间** | Turn on the light. / Turn the light on.<br>Turn it on. ✓ / Turn on it. ✗ |
| 及物、不可分 | 宾语只能放在小品词之后 | She looks after her sister.<br>She looks after her. ✓ / She looks her after. ✗ |
| 三个词 | 动词 + 副词 + 介词，不可分 | I'm looking forward to the trip. |

一个短语动词是否可分，需要查词典确认。词典中常用 sth / sb 的位置来表示：turn sth on 表示可分，look after sb 表示不可分。

短语动词的重音落在小品词上，如 give ˈup、take ˈoff，见[单词重音 · 复合词](../../pronunciation/stress/README.md#复合词)。

## 小品词的含义

同一个小品词在不同短语动词中往往带有相近的含义，掌握这些含义有助于猜测新短语的意思：

| 小品词 | 常见含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| up | 向上；增加；完成、彻底 | stand up、speed up、eat up、clean up、use up |
| down | 向下；减少；记录；停止、失灵 | sit down、slow down、write down、calm down、break down |
| out | 出去；出现；找到；完全耗尽 | go out、come out、find out、figure out、run out |
| off | 离开；断开、停止；取消、推迟 | take off、turn off、call off、put off |
| on | 继续；开启；穿上 | carry on、go on、turn on、put on |
| over | 越过；仔细地；从头到尾；结束 | get over、think over、take over |
| back | 返回；回应 | come back、call back、give back |
| away | 离开；消失 | go away、throw away、pass away |
| in | 进入；提交；加入 | come in、hand in、join in |

## 常用短语动词

### get

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| get up | 起床 | I get up at seven every day. |
| get along / on with | 与……相处 | Do you get along with your boss? |
| get over | 从（疾病、打击）中恢复 | It took her months to get over the loss. |
| get back | 回来；取回 | When did you get back? |
| get rid of | 摆脱，扔掉 | I need to get rid of these old clothes. |
| get through | 度过（难关）；接通（电话） | We'll get through this together. |

### take

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| take off | 起飞；脱下 | The plane took off on time.<br>Take off your shoes. |
| take after | 长得像（父母） | She takes after her mother. |
| take up | 开始从事（爱好）；占据 | He took up yoga last year. |
| take over | 接管 | Who will take over the project? |
| take back | 收回（说过的话）；退还 | I take back what I said. |

### put

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| put on | 穿上 | Put on your coat. |
| put off | 推迟 | The meeting was put off until Friday. |
| put up with | 忍受 | I can't put up with the noise any longer. |
| put away | 收好 | Put your toys away. |
| put out | 扑灭 | The firefighters put out the fire. |

### look

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| look after | 照顾 | Can you look after my cat? |
| look for | 寻找 | I'm looking for my keys. |
| look up | 查阅 | Look up the word in a dictionary. |
| look forward to | 期待 | I'm looking forward to seeing you. |
| look into | 调查 | The police are looking into the case. |
| look out | 小心 | Look out! A car is coming. |

look forward to 中的 to 是介词，后面接名词或动名词，不接动词原形。

### give

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| give up | 放弃 | Don't give up. |
| give in | 屈服，让步 | He finally gave in to their demands. |
| give back | 归还 | Give me back my phone. |
| give away | 赠送；泄露 | She gave away all her books. |

### turn

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| turn on / off | 打开 / 关闭（电器） | Turn off the TV. |
| turn up / down | 调大 / 调小（音量）；turn down 也指拒绝 | Turn down the music.<br>She turned down the offer. |
| turn out | 结果是 | It turned out to be a good idea. |
| turn into | 变成 | The caterpillar turned into a butterfly. |

### come

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| come up with | 想出（主意） | She came up with a great idea. |
| come across | 偶然遇到 | I came across an old photo. |
| come back | 回来 | Come back soon. |
| come out | 出版；出现 | His new book comes out next month. |

### go

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| go on | 继续 | Please go on. |
| go out | 外出；（灯、火）熄灭 | The lights went out. |
| go over | 复习，仔细检查 | Let's go over the plan again. |
| go through | 经历（困难）；仔细查看 | She's going through a hard time. |

### 其他

| 短语 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| break down | 出故障；崩溃 | My car broke down on the highway. |
| bring up | 抚养；提出（话题） | She was brought up by her grandparents. |
| call off | 取消 | The game was called off because of rain. |
| carry on | 继续 | Carry on with your work. |
| figure out | 弄明白，想出 | I can't figure out how it works. |
| find out | 查明，发现 | I'll find out when the train leaves. |
| fill in / out | 填写（表格） | Please fill in this form. |
| hang out | 闲逛，一起玩 | We usually hang out on weekends. |
| make up | 编造；化妆；和好 | He made up an excuse. |
| pick up | 捡起；接人；学会 | I'll pick you up at the airport. |
| run out of | 用完 | We've run out of milk. |
| set up | 建立，设立 | They set up a company in 2020. |
| show up | 出现，露面 | He didn't show up at the party. |
| work out | 锻炼；解决；算出 | I work out three times a week. |
